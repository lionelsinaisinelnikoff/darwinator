Why this process works. Research by Girotra, Terwiesch & Ulrich shows that a hybrid process (individuals generate ideas alone, then the group evaluates) produces significantly better best ideas than pure team brainstorming. It generates more ideas, higher average quality, and better discernment of the extremes. Interactive “build-up” in groups often produces weaker ideas on average.
